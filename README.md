# 🛒 Super Market Sales Dashboard

## 📖 Project Overview

This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of supermarket sales performance across different countries, markets, product categories, customer segments, and products. The dashboard helps stakeholders monitor sales trends, profitability, and business performance through interactive visualizations.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this dashboard is to analyze sales and profit performance, identify top-performing products and regions, and support data-driven business decisions.

---

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Value |
|------|------|
| Total Sales | 13M |
| Total Profit | 1.47M |
| Total Quantity Sold | 178K |

### Customer Insights

- Regional Performance Comparison

- Country-wise Filtering

## 💡 Key Insights

- Technology category contributes the highest share of total sales.
- United States generates the highest sales and profit among all countries.
- Certain products contribute significantly more profit than others.
- Monthly sales trends help identify seasonal demand patterns.
- Consumer segment represents a major share of revenue generation.
